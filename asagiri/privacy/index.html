隐私政策

朝雾（Asagiri Daily）· 生效日期：2026-10-29 · 最后更新：2026-10-29

一句话总结

朝雾不收集、不上传你的任何个人数据。所有记录只保存在你的设备上，开发者无法访问。

1. 我们收集哪些信息

不收集。朝雾没有账号系统、没有注册流程，也不包含任何第三方统计、分析或广告 SDK。你在 App 内记录的所有内容（晨间状态、睡眠、饮酒、运动、情绪、用药、备注、射精事件等）都只保存在本机。

2. 数据存储在哪里

记录使用 Apple 的 SwiftData 保存在你的设备本地。开发者没有服务器，也无法读取这些数据。卸载 App 或使用「更多 › 备份与恢复 › 删除全部记录」即可删除本机数据。

3. 设备权限

  • Face ID / 设备密码（可选）：仅用于打开 App 时在本机验证身份，由 iOS 系统处理，朝雾无法读取你的生物识别数据。该功能默认关闭。
  • 通知（可选）：仅用于「晨间提醒」，是本地通知，不经过任何推送服务器。通知内容只有一句提醒，不含你的记录内容。该功能默认关闭。
  • 相册 / 文件分享：只有当你主动点击「导出 Excel」「分享 PDF 报告」并选择保存或发送时，数据才会通过系统分享面板离开 App；去向完全由你决定。

4. 数据共享

我们不共享、不出售、不转让你的任何数据，因为开发者根本接触不到这些数据。App 使用 Apple 提供的系统能力（TestFlight、App Store、系统分享面板等），这些服务由 Apple 按其隐私政策处理。

5. 未来功能

当前版本不包含 iCloud 同步或云端备份。若未来提供可选的 iCloud 备份，会在开启前明确告知并更新本政策；在用户主动开启之前不会上传任何数据。

6. 儿童

朝雾面向成年用户，不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的信息。App 不提供任何账号或社交功能。

7. 你的权利

由于数据只存储在你的设备上，你随时可以在 App 内查看、修改或删除它，也可以直接卸载 App 来清除全部数据。如果你对本政策有疑问，请联系我们。

8. 政策变更

如果本政策发生变更，我们会更新本页面并修改「最后更新」日期；涉及数据处理方式的重大变更会在 App 内提示。

Privacy Policy (English)

Asagiri Daily · Effective: October 29, 2026 · Last updated: October 29, 2026

Summary

Asagiri Daily does not collect or upload your data. Everything you record stays on your device, and the developer cannot access it.

1. Information we collect

None. There is no account system, no sign-up, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. Everything you enter in the app (morning condition, sleep, alcohol, exercise, mood, medication, notes, ejaculation events) is stored locally on your device.

2. Where data is stored

Records are stored with Apple's SwiftData on your device. The developer has no server and cannot read your data. You can delete local data in the app (More › Backup & Restore › Delete all records) or by deleting the app.

3. Device permissions

  • Face ID / device passcode (optional): used only to unlock the app locally. Biometrics are handled by iOS; the app cannot read your biometric data. Off by default.
  • Notifications (optional): used only for the morning reminder, delivered locally with no push server. The notification text contains no record data. Off by default.
  • Files and sharing: data leaves the app only when you tap “Export Excel” or “Share PDF report” and choose a destination in the system share sheet. You decide where it goes.

4. Data sharing

We do not share, sell or transfer your data, because the developer never has access to it. The app uses Apple system services (TestFlight, the App Store, the system share sheet), which are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

5. Future features

This version does not include iCloud sync or cloud backup. If an optional iCloud backup is added later, we will disclose it before it is enabled and update this policy; no data will be uploaded unless you explicitly turn it on.

6. Children

Asagiri Daily is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. The app has no account or social features and does not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Your choices

Since data lives only on your device, you can view, edit or delete it in the app at any time, or delete the app to remove everything. Contact us if you have questions.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “last updated” date; significant changes to data handling will be surfaced in the app.
